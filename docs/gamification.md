# Gamification & Rewards

## Objective
Encourage safe driving and increase customer engagement.

## Features
1. **Safe Streaks**
   - Consecutive safe trips earn reward points.
   - Discounts applied at renewal.

2. **Leaderboards**
   - Compare safe driving scores across peers.
   - Promotes friendly competition.

3. **Badges**
   - Bronze, Silver, Gold tiers for safe drivers.

4. **Premium Discounts**
   - Reward points converted to real premium discounts.

## Business Impact
- Reduces churn by keeping drivers engaged.
- Encourages adoption of safer habits → fewer claims.
- Strengthens insurer–customer relationship.
