# Dynamic Premiums & ROI

## Premium Types
1. **Flat Premium**  
   - Fixed base rate (e.g., $1000/year).
   - Ignores driver behavior.

2. **ML-Adjusted Premium**  
   - Scales premium by traditional ML risk score.
   - Example: RandomForest adds 10–30% based on events.

3. **AI-Driven Premium**  
   - Uses Transformer risk probabilities.
   - Captures nuanced patterns (e.g., consistent mild speeding).

## Example (Synthetic Drivers)
| Driver         | Flat Premium | ML Premium | AI Premium |
|----------------|--------------|------------|------------|
| Grace Williams | $877         | $1360      | $1227      |
| Ivy Williams   | $1046        | $1492      | $1710      |
| Frank Smith    | $1168        | $1581      | $1846      |

## ROI Impact
- Safer drivers rewarded with discounts → reduced churn.
- Risky drivers charged higher premiums → improved portfolio stability.
- Gamification incentives reduce claims cost over time.
